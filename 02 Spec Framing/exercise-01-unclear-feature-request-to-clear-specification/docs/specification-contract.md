# Spec Kit artifact contract

Paths below are relative to the exercise directory. The feature directory is `specs/subscription-management/`.

## Native specification

Keep Spec Kit's spec.md structure, including User Scenarios & Testing, Requirements, Success Criteria, and Assumptions. Retain prioritized user stories, FR identifiers, observable Given/When/Then acceptance scenarios, and SC identifiers. Reference the relevant FR identifiers in acceptance scenarios so coverage can be checked.

Add short `## Scope` and `## Readiness` sections. Scope states included and excluded work. Readiness contains:
- `Status:` Ready or Needs decisions.
- `Blocked:` comma-separated FR identifiers, or None.
- `Conditional:` comma-separated FR identifiers, or None.

Prefix those fields with `- `. Requirements supported by unanswered questions must be Blocked; those supported by provisional assumptions must be Conditional or Blocked. Every unblocked FR needs at least one acceptance scenario. Include at least one testable scenario overall. Keep unresolved `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: ...]` markers when appropriate; never convert uncertainty into approval to satisfy a checklist.

## Clarification ledger

Keep `clarifications.md` alongside the native spec. Use unique `## Q1: ...?` headings, with single-line fields prefixed `- `:

- `Category:` Authorization, Billing, Pending, Recovery, or Scope; multiple categories are allowed.
- `Requirements:` the native FR identifiers affected.
- `Repository evidence:` application source/docs paths and line references.
- `Status:` Confirmed, Assumption, or Open.
- `Answer:`, `Authority:`, and `Consequence:`.
- `Owner:` and `Next step:` for Assumption/Open decisions.

Cover all five categories and consult stakeholder notes, billing constraints, and source code. Every FR must trace to at least one question. There is no fixed question quota. Technical capability and unapproved stakeholder notes do not establish future product policy. If clarify reaches its interaction limit, retain remaining questions as Open rather than inventing answers.

## Checklist and fresh review

The custom `checklists/spec-review.md` is generated by checklist, distinct from specify's built-in `checklists/requirements.md`. Before review every custom item must be unchecked and have a unique CHK identifier.

Freeze the spec, decision ledger, built-in checklist, and custom checklist. The fresh reviewer returns:
- `reviewed-checklist.md`: the same CHK items and wording, with checked items only where the requirements satisfy the item.
- `checklist-results.json`: `items` containing every CHK ID exactly once, each with `status` (Satisfied, Gap, or Not applicable), `reason`, and `references` (FR/SC/Q identifiers or Scope). A Gap also references a REV finding through `finding`.
- `review.md`: Coverage and Findings sections, addressing all five categories. Findings use `### REV-001: ...` with Severity (Blocking/Non-blocking), Location (draft FR/SC/Q ID or Scope), and Concern. Prefix fields `- `.

The fresh agent is explicitly asked to assess the checklist; generation alone is insufficient. Satisfied items are checked; Gap and Not applicable items remain unchecked with explanations. Preserve the original reviewer output. A review with no actionable findings may say so explicitly; do not manufacture findings.

## Revisions and readiness

In `revisions.md`, give every REV finding its own matching heading with Disposition (Addressed, Deferred, Rejected) and Reason. Addressed findings need Change: a final FR/SC/Q identifier or Scope whose content changed. Deferred findings need Owner and Next step.

Retain the original reviewed checklist. Final `checklist-results.json` may add `resolution` for a Gap: an addressed/rejected REV disposition explains the outcome; a deferred gap remains open. Do not rewrite the reviewer's initial assessment to make it look successful.

Overall readiness must be Needs decisions while any question is unconfirmed, any FR is Blocked/Conditional, any clarification marker remains, or a blocking finding is deferred. Exercise completion permits honest blockers; readiness is a separate assessment.
