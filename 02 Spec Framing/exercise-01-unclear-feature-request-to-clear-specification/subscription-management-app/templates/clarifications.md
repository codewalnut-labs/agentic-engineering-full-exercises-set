# Clarifications

Create this ledger at `specs/subscription-management/clarifications.md`, alongside the native Spec Kit spec. Keep the native Clarifications section in spec.md; this ledger adds source provenance and decision dependencies.

## Q1: What decision needs an answer?

- Category: Authorization, Billing, Pending, Recovery, or Scope
- Requirements: FR identifiers affected by this decision
- Repository evidence: Cite subscription-management-app/docs/ or src/ paths and lines.
- Status: Confirmed, Assumption, or Open
- Answer: Supported answer, provisional proposal, or decision still needed.
- Authority: Verified repository fact or actual stakeholder answer.
- Consequence: What this enables, changes, or blocks.
- Owner: Required for Assumption or Open.
- Next step: Required for Assumption or Open.

Cover all five categories. Do not turn agent suggestions or unapproved notes into confirmed product policy.
