# Evidence instructions and template

Use actual captured results. evidence/before.md describes the starting state and evidence/after.md describes the measured result. Neither file is an invented alternate implementation. Use these headings in both:

    ## Conditions
    Starting commit: <full SHA>
    Implementation commit: <full SHA, after.md only>
    ## Findings
    <observations, numbers, failures, and limitations>
    ## Proof
    <capture paths and exact source or transcript lines>

Use Changes, Verified, and Remaining questions headings in evidence/comparison.md. Retain every failed attempt and disclose retries and human assistance. The fixed two-lane workload, not a hand-picked best run, is the comparison.

## Required artifacts

- evidence/records.json indexes the complete before and after captures.
- evidence/raw/ contains original bytes. Commit every indexed file; the index records SHA-256 digests and verification checks both the bytes and the committed evidence snapshot.
- evidence/summary.json is generated by experiment:score; do not edit it.
- evidence/decision.md uses Measurements, Correctness, Decision, and Limitations headings. Its Decision: adopt or Decision: reject line must match the score. Explain what the numbers measure and what they do not.
- evidence/commands/baseline.txt and evidence/commands/implementation.txt are recorded at their declared source commits with proof:capture.
- evidence/manifest.json seals the reports and index after committing the complete evidence.
- evidence/commands/verify.txt is captured after sealing and is not included in its own inputs.

## Source audit

Create evidence/source-audit.json with a claims array. Cover topics measurement, correctness, and limits. Each claim has an id, topic, status (supported, contradicted, or unresolved), reason, artifact {path,line,excerpt}, and sources [{path,line,excerpt}]. Use one-based lines and exact excerpts. The artifact must point into evidence/decision.md or evidence/summary.json. Sources may use docs/, rtk-investigation-app/src/, rtk-investigation-app/tests/, rtk-investigation-app/fixtures/, evidence/raw/, or evidence/commands/. Submitted reports cannot support themselves. Cite actual command/response lines for numerical claims and acceptance results for correctness. A human checks that the source supports the claim.
